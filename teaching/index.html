Teaching, Coursework & Mentoring

I am committed to equipping life scientists with computational autonomy, command-line confidence, and rigorous algorithmic reasoning. My instructional career spans formal graduate and undergraduate university courses, intensive hands-on workshops, and individualized research mentorship.

Educational Philosophy

Pedagogical Philosophy & Educational Vision

Bridging abstract biological concepts with hands-on computational execution. I design courses and workshops that treat command-line proficiency, reproducible version control, and rigorous algorithmic reasoning as core competencies for modern life scientists.

Principle 01

Active, Terminal-First Learning

Every lecture is paired with real-world computational problem sets where students analyze actual sequencing data rather than synthetic toy examples.

Principle 02

Reproducibility as a Core Value

Teaching Git version control, containerized environments (Docker/Singularity), and workflow orchestrators (Nextflow) from day one.

Principle 03

Inclusive Mentorship & Trainee Ownership

Empowering students through transparent authorship compacts, weekly 1-on-1 milestone tracking, and individualized development plans (IDPs).
